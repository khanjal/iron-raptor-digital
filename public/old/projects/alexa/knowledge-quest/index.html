Knowledge Quest

Published January 19, 2018 · Last updated January 19, 2018

Knowledge Quest is an interactive game where you pick a topic, explore, and then get a quiz on what you’ve learned based on the difficulty you select.

Description

Knowledge Quest allows you to choose your adventure to explore places, both real and mythical, to learn facts about them. At the end of the adventure, you receive a quiz based on the facts you learned with the amount of questions and answers determined by the difficulty you select.

Each adventure is randomized with locations and facts, ensuring you will have a different experience each time even if you pick the same adventure and visit the same locations.

If you have any suggestions for adventures or locations, feel free to contact us at IronRaptorDigital@gmail.com
