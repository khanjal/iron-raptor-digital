Adventure Craft

Published November 26, 2017 · Last updated November 26, 2017

Explore the world of Minecraft on Alexa with this unofficial interactive adventure story.

Accomplishments

  • Get to the Nether

 

Features

  • Explore the world of Minecraft through story
  • Mine, craft, and build in locations in the story
  • Options are presented at every area but there are some secret ones.

 

Latest Updates – October 23, 2017

  • Descriptions are no longer read when you revisit an area.
  • Some new scenes that you can visit and will be used for further progression in the future.
  • Finding the nether no longer ends the game.
  • You can craft and build some additional items.
  • Hints will display for certain scenes.

FAQ

What are scenes?

Scenes are what we call locations or areas in the world that you can visit. Each scene can have several actions which show or hide depending on what you have in inventory or what you have built in that scene.

 

How many scenes are there?

For each accomplishment there are about 6 scenes. Some of these are reused depending on what is needed for the accomplishment.

 

Can I build wherever I want?

We have predefined places where structures, mobs, and items can be built. This is to keep some scenes from getting too complex.

 

When I die, why doesn’t my stuff drop?

We thought about having your stuff drop at the scene where you died, but eventually you could be dozens of scenes away from it and that would make it difficult to get back to. For the sake of fun, you keep your items when you die. We issued the “/gamerule keepInventory true” command.

 

What are encounters?

Randomly in the world you’ll run across mobs like creepers, skeletons, zombies, spiders, etc… Encounters allow you to fight or avoid these mobs.

 

How much can my inventory hold?

For the sake of fun, your inventory can hold what you need when you need it. When you harvest wood, you have enough wood for whenever you need it. Same with stone, iron, etc…

 

How is item or armor durability handled?

Durability is ignored, but items could be destroyed in unconventional ways, and possibly even given away.

 

How do I make crafting tables, furnaces, and other tools?

Items like crafting tables and furnaces are automatically crafted and placed when needed.
